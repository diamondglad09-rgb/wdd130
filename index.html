About Nigeria

Nigeria is a vibrant and culturally diverse west african nation often referred to as the Giant of Africa that boasts the continent's largest population, thriving economy driven by trade and energy, and a massive global cultural influence through its world-famous Afrobeats music and Nollywood cinema and rich culinary traditions. Home to more than 250 distinct ethnic groups most notably the Hausa-Fulani, Yoruba, and Igbo it features a uniquely dynamic landscape that spans from humid coastal lagoons anchored by the sprawling megacity of Lagos to central savannas surrounding the capital city of Abuja, and northern plans bordering the Sahara Desert.
